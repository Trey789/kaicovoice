Why search rank is not your front door.

The 70 to 85 percent figure is industry data (PushLeads), not a Kaico result, and it explains something every restoration owner already knows: the job comes from the adjuster, the carrier program and the plumber who was there first, far more often than from a Google search. "Water damage restoration marketing" is searched about 390 times a month across the whole country (Semrush, September 2026). That is not where the work is decided.

The work is decided by the weather and by who answers. Oklahoma ranked 3rd in the nation for hailstorms in 2024, with 767, 1st for tornadoes, with 151, and 5th in State Farm hail payouts in 2025 (Carrier Management; Live Insurance News). The season is predictable. The call at 2am is not, and it goes to whoever picks up: you, the shop across town, or the national franchise with a call center.

A referral works the same way. The plumber standing in the flooded kitchen hands the homeowner a name. If that homeowner Googles the name and finds a listing with no emergency hours and a site with a dead contact form, the referral dies on the spot and the plumber stops making it. Your front door is what the referrer's customer sees in the first sixty seconds.

What a midnight call sounds like.

An illustration of the flow, not a transcript

It is 12:15 on a Sunday night. A homeowner calls your number: the dishwasher supply line failed, the kitchen floor is under an inch of water and it is running into the hall. The line picks up on the first ring with your company name, and the agent asks what is going on.

It asks the questions you gave it: is the water stopped (she found the valve under the sink), where did it come from, has she called her insurer yet, and who the carrier is. It collects her name, address and callback number. Because your rule for an active loss is dispatch, it transfers the call to your on-call tech with everything already captured, and the transcript is on your phone before the truck starts.

If the water had been stopped for hours and nobody was in danger, it would have booked the first real opening onto your calendar instead, and texted her a confirmation.
