Brand and program

Brand Kaico LLC (Kaico Voice is a service of Kaico LLC)
Program name Kaico Voice texting
What you'll receive (1) Replies from Serenity, Kaico Voice's AI assistant, when you text (918) 359-2644 first: answers about our services and help booking a 15-minute intro call. Cost Message and data rates may apply, depending on your wireless carrier and plan. Kaico Voice does not charge you for SMS messages.
Supported carriers All major US carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, US Cellular) plus most regional carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How you opt in

  1. Conversation texts: you text Kaico Voice at (918) 359-2644 to ask a question or request an appointment. That first message is your request for a reply, and we reply only in the conversation you started. Our first reply names Kaico Voice and our assistant and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
  2. Appointment texts: after you book an intro call in that conversation, our assistant asks: "Kaico Voice can text you a reminder the day before, up to 3 texts for this booking. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply YES to opt in, STOP to opt out." Only a YES (or SI) in reply to that question signs you up, for that booking only. Your booking stands whether or not you reply YES.

Calling us, leaving a voicemail or filling in a website form does not sign you up for texts.
