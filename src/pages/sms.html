How you opt in

  1. Conversation replies: you text Kaico Voice at (918) 359-2644 to ask a question or request an appointment. We reply only in the conversation you started. Our first reply names Kaico Voice and our assistant and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."
  2. Appointment texts: after you book an intro call by text, our assistant asks: "Kaico Voice can text you a reminder the day before, up to 3 texts for this booking. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply YES to opt in, STOP to opt out." Only a YES (or SI) in reply signs you up, for that booking only. Your booking stands either way.
  3. Callback follow-ups: when you leave a callback request on a call, our assistant asks: "Can Kaico Voice text you at this number about this request, up to 2 texts? Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP at any time." Only a yes signs you up, for that request only.
  4. Replies, quotes and project updates: our Get Started form has an optional box, unticked unless you tick it: "Text me about my request at this number. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." You can also say yes on a call or text us first.
  5. Client account texts: clients choose texts in the SMS section of their client agreement or onboarding form, which names each kind of text (lead alerts, project updates, quotes, invoices, receipts and one payment reminder per invoice). It is optional and not a condition of service.

Calling us, leaving a voicemail or sending the form without the text box ticked does not sign you up for texts. We keep a record of each opt-in: how you opted in and when.
