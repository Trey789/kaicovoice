Frequently asked.

Does this store my hours?

No. The checker runs in your browser and sends nothing to Kaico or anyone else. Reload the page and it goes back to the default week.

What counts as covered?

An hour counts as covered when you mark it as a time someone at the shop answers. The checker does not know whether that person actually picked up; it only counts the hours you enter.

What about holidays?

Not modeled. The checker assumes every week looks the same. Holidays, sick days and a tech out on a long job are not in the count.

Is it accurate?

It is arithmetic on the hours you enter, nothing more: 168 hours in a week, minus the hours you mark as answered. If the shop line forwards to a cell after closing, enter those hours too.

How are nights and weekend days counted?

A night runs from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM and counts as uncovered when nobody answers at any point in it. A weekend day counts when Saturday or Sunday has no answered hours at all.
