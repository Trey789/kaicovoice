Electrician answering service questions.

What does an electrician answering service do?

It receives calls for an electrical business while the office is closed or the team is occupied. Kaico uses an AI receptionist to collect the caller details and request, book available Google Calendar appointments under your rules, or route the request to your team. It identifies itself as AI.

How much is the electrician answering service?

The phone plan is $299 a month plus a one-time $299 setup, month to month with 30 days written notice to cancel. Front Door starts at $900 a month and adds website care, Google Business Profile work and the job system. Confirm capacity and usage terms before buying.

Will the assistant diagnose an electrical fault?

The workflow is for intake, approved service information and routing. Technical diagnosis, repair instructions, electrical safety decisions and emergency dispatch are outside this phone service. Your qualified team handles technical judgment.

How are urgent calls handed to a person?

You choose the transfer number, escalation rules and fallback process. The assistant can attempt a transfer or capture details for follow-up. A transfer depends on your person being available; Kaico is not supplying a staffed human backup or guaranteeing an emergency response.

Can it book panel upgrades, EV chargers or inspections?

It can collect requests for services you offer and book available Google Calendar slots for appointment types you approve. A site visit or estimate request should be distinguished from a confirmed installation. Technician assignment, job duration, permits and inspection requirements need your team and approved scheduling rules.

Does it integrate with my field-service software?

Google Calendar is the documented scheduling connection. A native ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro or other field-service integration is not included by default. Confirm the handoff and any separate scope before launch.

Can it take overflow calls and speak Spanish?

Call forwarding can be configured for the coverage you need after your provider settings are confirmed and tested. The public demo is in English; another phone language needs a separate confirmation and demonstration. Confirm peak and simultaneous-call capacity rather than assuming unlimited calls.

We are not quoting verified electrical bookings, revenue gains or dispatch outcomes. The Blue Sands Trucking website, calculator and scheduler show delivered web work for a hauling business.
