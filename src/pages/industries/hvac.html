HVAC answering service questions.

How much does the HVAC answering service cost?

The phone service is $299 a month plus a one-time $299 setup. It is month to month, with 30 days written notice to cancel. Front Door starts at $900 a month and includes the receptionist alongside website care, Google Business Profile work and the job system. See the pricing page for the full scope.

Can it book HVAC calls after hours?

It can check availability and book appointments into your connected Google Calendar at night and on weekends, using the service area, appointment types and hours you approve. Booking depends on available slots and the scheduling rules tested during setup.

Does it integrate with ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Google Calendar booking is the documented scheduling connection. A native integration with your field-service platform is not included by default. Tell us which system you use so we can confirm the handoff and any separate integration scope before you buy.

What happens when a caller needs a person?

You choose the transfer number and escalation rules. The assistant can attempt a transfer or capture a message for your team. Your team provides the human coverage; a successful transfer depends on someone being available. This service does not provide emergency dispatch or a staffed human backup team.

Are calls unlimited?

The published phone price is $299 a month plus $299 setup. It is not a promise of unlimited capacity or a guaranteed answer time. Bring your expected monthly volume and busiest-hour demand to the demo so capacity, simultaneous calls and any usage terms can be confirmed in writing before you buy.

Can it answer in Spanish?

The public phone demo is in English. A Spanish website does not establish Spanish phone support. If you need another language, ask us to confirm and demonstrate it for your setup before you buy.

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes. Callers are told they are speaking with an AI. During setup we agree on the greeting, approved service information, questions to ask and when your team should take over.

Do you have verified HVAC customer results?

We are not publishing a verified HVAC booking or revenue result here. Our Blue Sands Trucking example shows delivered website work, including a pricing calculator and self-serve scheduler. It is a hauling business, not an HVAC case study. Use the demo to test your own call scenarios and read our proof page for what has been verified.
