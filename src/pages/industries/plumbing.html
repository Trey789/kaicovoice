Plumbing answering service questions.

What is a plumbing answering service?

It handles incoming calls for a plumbing business when the office is closed or the team is busy. Kaico uses an AI receptionist to collect service details, check available Google Calendar appointments and follow the routing rules you approve. Callers are told they are speaking with an AI.

How much does the plumbing answering service cost?

The phone plan is $299 a month plus a one-time $299 setup. It is month to month, with 30 days written notice to cancel. Front Door starts at $900 a month and adds website care, Google Business Profile work and the job system. Confirm volume, simultaneous-call capacity and any usage terms before buying.

Can it handle an urgent leak call after hours?

You approve the questions, transfer number and escalation rules. The assistant can collect the request and attempt a transfer to your chosen person. Your team supplies the human coverage. The service does not provide emergency dispatch, repair advice or a guaranteed technician arrival.

Can it book into Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan?

The documented booking connection is Google Calendar. Native field-service integrations are not included by default. Tell us which system your dispatcher uses so the handoff and any separate integration scope can be confirmed before you buy.

Can it quote a drain cleaning or water-heater job?

The script can include fees you have approved, such as a service-call fee. Work that needs an inspection or a custom estimate should become a quote request for your team. A calendar appointment is not a confirmed repair price or a promise that a technician has been dispatched.

Can I keep my number and answer calls myself?

The service uses call forwarding from your existing number. We need to confirm and test your provider settings, when calls should forward and the fallback path before launch. Your own team can remain the first point of contact.

Does it answer in Spanish or offer unlimited calls?

The public phone demo is in English. Other language support must be confirmed and demonstrated for your setup. The published price does not promise unlimited capacity or a guaranteed answer time; capacity and usage terms need written confirmation.

Do you have verified plumbing customer results?

We are not publishing verified plumbing bookings or revenue outcomes here. Blue Sands Trucking shows delivered website work, including a pricing calculator and self-serve scheduler. It is a hauling business, not proof of plumbing call-handling results. Test your own workflow in the demo and review our proof page.
