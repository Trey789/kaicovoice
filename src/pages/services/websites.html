What is broken today.

"Contractor website design" is searched about 1,600 times a month in the United States, and the sites those searches produce keep shipping with the same three holes. We saw every one of them in the Tulsa audits we ran this month.

The phone number is plain text.

It is on the page, it looks fine on a laptop, and on a phone it cannot be tapped. The customer who wanted to call you now has to remember eleven digits and switch apps. Most do not.

The quote form never asks for a phone.

A foundation company we audited had the best review profile in its trade and a form that collected a name and a message. When someone did reach out, there was no way to call them back.

The site still loads over plain http.

Chrome and Safari flag it "Not Secure" before a visitor sees a single word about your work. One of the highest-ranked HVAC companies in Tulsa is losing clicks to that warning right now.
